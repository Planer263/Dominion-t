A FIELD GUIDE TO AMBITION

Start small. Think bigger.

01 / Claim your start

During deployment, click land to place your capital. Click another spot to change it before the timer ends. Keep some distance from rivals.

02 / Expand deliberately

Choose Expand and click nearby neutral land or an enemy nation sharing your border. A campaign spends your chosen troop percentage and pushes toward the clicked location. Stop returns half its remaining army.

03 / Build your advantage

Click your own land with City, Fort, or Port selected. Cities grow income and troop capacity. Forts protect tiles within six cells. Coastal ports generate gold and unlock transports.

04 / Make a landing

Build a port, choose Land, then click any coastal land tile. A visible transport carries your committed troops from the nearest port to a new beachhead. It may cross land visually; there is no naval pathfinding or ship combat.

05 / Diplomacy is a tool

Select a nation and offer a 60-second truce. Accept incoming requests from its intelligence panel. Truces block attacks and landings between you. You can also gift 20% of your troops during a truce.

06 / Leave a mark

Reach 65% of land or be the last surviving nation. Losing your original capital doesn’t eliminate you—losing all land does. Islands require transports. Hover for terrain and ownership; select to inspect.
